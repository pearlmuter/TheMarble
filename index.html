About this view

This is a scientific illustration, not a live camera image. Sunlight scatters and is absorbed in a simplified Earth atmosphere. Brightness is adjusted so the Sun, stars, and night-side details remain readable together.

Cloud relief is illustrative; cloud edges are gently smoothed for display. Polar cloud coverage is incomplete, and an undrawn area does not mean clear skies. Sea ice uses measured concentration where available, with ice-presence analysis elsewhere; these are not observations of individual floes.

Aurora is a NOAA SWPC forecast illustration. Its field-aligned curtains and light output are physical approximations, with night-view exposure; forecast times and demonstration controls are in Debug view.

Atmospheric conditions vary in reality. This model does not yet include the bending of light that shifts and flattens the Sun at the horizon, so it should not be used to teach exact apparent sunrise timing.
